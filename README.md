The Price of a Promise

A Journey Through Deception, Loss, Recovery, and Hope

Some promises cost more than money.

The Price of a Promise tells the story of a devastating romance scam and the far reaching consequences that followed. What began as trust placed in the wrong person became a journey through financial loss, emotional devastation, difficult lessons, recovery, and ultimately healing.

Beyond the transactions, investigations, and consequences lies a deeper story about vulnerability, hope, resilience, faith, and the strength required to rebuild after betrayal.

This book explores the human side of deception while offering encouragement to those navigating loss, regret, shame, broken trust, and the long road toward restoration.
